## Caption pattern

1. Start with the recognizable moment or exact sentence.
2. Give one distinction or practical change.
3. Include one example the viewer can save.
4. End with one action: practise at `www.baatdilse.com`.

Do not use long blocks of hashtags. Use three to six accurate tags per post. Suggested rotation:

`#BaatDilSe #DilSePakistan #RelationshipCommunication #UrduContent #PakistaniWomen #MarriageConversations`

## Community responses

- Do not diagnose a follower’s relationship in comments.
- Do not ask for names, addresses, phone numbers or identifying details.
- For ordinary wording requests, offer a general sentence structure and direct the person to DilSe for private practice.
- For threats, physical harm, blocked exits or immediate danger, direct the person to local emergency support and the Pakistan safety page. Do not direct urgent situations into a routine chat CTA.
