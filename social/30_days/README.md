# DilSe 30-day social package

This folder contains the finished content for a 30-day DilSe launch campaign.

## Included

- 30 daily content folders under `assets/`
- 66 feed, carousel and editorial graphics at 1080 × 1350 px
- 17 reel-cover and story graphics at 1080 × 1920 px
- 13 animation frames and four finished looping GIFs at 540 × 960 px
- complete captions and alt text in `PUBLISHING_COPY.md`
- eight shot-by-shot TikTok and Instagram Reel scripts in `TIKTOK_REELS_SCRIPTS.md`
- Instagram and TikTok profile, hashtag and community guidance in `PLATFORM_GUIDE.md`
- sticker instructions for four story sequences
- four Urdu feed posts with properly joined Urdu script
- Urdu and Roman Urdu short-video scripts
- two new editorial illustrations and finished feed layouts
- six distinct campaign layouts instead of one repeated card template
- `campaign-preview.png` showing the lead asset for every day
- `carousel-preview.png` showing the complete carousel sets
- final verification results in `QUALITY_CHECK.md`

## Publishing workflow

1. Open the folder for the correct day.
2. Upload every numbered slide or story frame in filename order.
3. Copy the matching caption from `PUBLISHING_COPY.md`.
4. Add the specified poll, quiz, question, slider, or link sticker inside the social platform.
5. For reel days, use the supplied vertical cover and the matching script. The GIF supplies an animated preview for days 3, 9, 16 and 23.
6. Confirm that the profile bio and story link point to `www.baatdilse.com`.

## Production note

Reel covers, scripts and four looping GIF previews are complete. Final TikTok and Reel MP4 files still require recorded footage or an approved text-only motion treatment, voiceover and licensed audio.

Review Urdu copy with a native Pakistani Urdu speaker before using it in paid advertising.
