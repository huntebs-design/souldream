## Weekly operating routine

### Sunday review

At 6:00 p.m. PKT each Sunday:

1. Confirm the next seven scheduled posts are still present.
2. Check that carousel slides remain in numerical order.
3. Confirm every caption ends with the intended website action.
4. Check that the correct cover is selected for each Reel.
5. Review comments that need a general wording response or safety redirection.

### Daily checks

- **1:15 p.m. PKT:** Confirm the scheduled Story published and its link opens `www.baatdilse.com`.
- **8:40 p.m. PKT:** Confirm the Instagram feed post published correctly.
- **9:10 p.m. PKT:** Confirm the TikTok post published correctly.
- **Following morning:** Reply to relevant comments without requesting personal or identifying details.

## Scheduling order

Schedule the campaign one week at a time for the first month. Queue days 1–7, review how the formats display, then queue the next seven days. This makes it easier to correct cropping, Urdu rendering or platform-specific caption limits before the same issue affects the entire month.

Instagram requires a professional account for native scheduled posts and Reels. Keep the browser signed in until the scheduling session is complete. Stories containing polls, questions, sliders or link stickers may require manual placement in the Instagram app even when the underlying graphic is prepared.
