# DilSe TikTok and Instagram Reels scripts

These eight videos are designed for both TikTok and Instagram Reels. Record vertically at 1080 × 1920. Keep spoken lines natural, add burned-in captions, and keep the website visible during the final two seconds.

## Video 1: Turn a complaint into a request

**Length:** 14 seconds  
**Language:** English with Roman Urdu alternative  
**Hook on screen:** “You never help.” What should I say instead?

**Shot list**

1. 0–2 seconds: A note on a phone reads, “You never help.” A hand deletes it.
2. 2–7 seconds: Type, “Can you handle school notices, forms, fees and follow-up this month?”
3. 7–11 seconds: Show four words in sequence: “Task. Owner. Deadline. Follow-up.”
4. 11–14 seconds: DilSe screen or branded end card. “Practise your version at baatdilse.com.”

**Voiceover:** “A complaint shows frustration. A request names what needs to change. Practise the sentence before the real conversation.”

**Roman Urdu option:** “Shikayat gussa batati hai. Clear request yeh batati hai ke badalna kya hai.”

**Caption:** “Help more” can still leave one person planning and reminding. Name the complete responsibility. Find words that fit your home at www.baatdilse.com.  
**Cover:** What do I even say?  
**Alt text:** A vague complaint is rewritten as a specific request about school responsibilities.

## Recording rules

- Keep the first spoken or written hook within the first second.
- Use captions large enough to read without sound.
- Keep important text inside the central 80% of the frame so platform controls do not cover it.
- Use ordinary home settings and hands, notes, tea, calendars or phone screens when nobody wants to appear on camera.
- Do not show a user’s real DilSe conversation or identifying details.
- Use quiet original audio or properly licensed music. Do not rely on trending audio to carry the message.
- End routine videos with `www.baatdilse.com`. End immediate-safety content with the Pakistan safety page or local emergency support.
