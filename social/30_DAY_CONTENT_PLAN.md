# DilSe 30-day social content plan

## Campaign objective

Help women in Pakistan recognize a conversation they have been postponing, show how DilSe can help them prepare, and send them to **www.baatdilse.com** to begin.

Primary action on every post: **Visit www.baatdilse.com and start a conversation.**

## Content mix

- **Recognizable moments:** household work, family boundaries, money, trust, intimacy, parenting, and emotional distance.
- **Words to practise:** specific conversation openers that readers can save and adapt.
- **How DilSe works:** The Listener, The Partner, language choice, exercises, and feedback.
- **Pakistani context:** family involvement, respect, joint and nuclear households, privacy concerns, and the fact that relationships in Pakistan are not all the same.
- **Participation:** polls, question boxes, and saveable prompts that lead naturally to a DilSe conversation.

## Posting rhythm

- Publish feed content once per day for the 30-day launch period.
- Add two or three supporting story frames on reel and carousel days.
- Pin days 1, 4, and 11 so new visitors immediately understand the audience, modes, and workflow.
- Reuse days 7, 12, 21, and 28 as a continuing **Words to practise** series after launch.
- Put **www.baatdilse.com** in the profile bio and use a link sticker in every story that contains a direct invitation.

## Production guidance

- Use the maroon, cream, rose, teal, and saffron palette already established in the product.
- Use one strong statement per static post. Put explanation in the caption rather than shrinking body text.
- Keep Urdu right-aligned and use a proper Urdu font. Do not place Urdu text using software that breaks letter joining.
- Add English alt text to every post, including Urdu posts.
- Avoid claims that DilSe provides therapy, guarantees confidentiality, fixes relationships, or replaces emergency and professional services.
- Review every Urdu caption with a native Pakistani Urdu speaker before paid promotion.
