## Post 1: Who DilSe is for

**Format:** Static feed post

**Caption**

Some conversations stay in your head because you cannot find a way to begin.

DilSe is an AI relationship guidance and conversation-practice app for women in Pakistan. Use it to think through a concern, prepare a specific sentence, or practise a difficult conversation before having it in real life.

Start with the part you keep replaying. You do not need a finished explanation.

Visit www.baatdilse.com and start a conversation.

## Post 2: The Listener and The Partner

**Format:** Static feed post

**Caption**

Use The Listener when you need to slow down, sort through what happened, and decide what you want to say.

Use The Partner when you want to rehearse the conversation and hear how another person might respond. Revise the words and try again before speaking to the real person.

Choose the mode that fits what you need at www.baatdilse.com.

**Alt text:** DilSe graphic comparing two modes. The Listener helps the user sort through a concern, while The Partner helps the user rehearse a conversation.

## Post 3: How DilSe works

**Format:** Six-slide carousel

**Caption**

Start with one message. Choose whether you want to talk the concern through or rehearse the real conversation.

DilSe asks one focused question at a time, helps clarify what you need, and can suggest wording for you to review. You decide which words fit your circumstances and voice.

DilSe provides guidance and practice. It does not provide licensed therapy or emergency support.

Begin at www.baatdilse.com.
