# DilSe social content quality check

Final review completed after the campaign redesign.

## Scope

- 81 graphics in the 30-day campaign
- 8 graphics in the original launch set
- 89 graphics reviewed in total

## Verified

- All 64 feed and carousel graphics are 1080 × 1350 px.
- All 17 reel-cover and story graphics are 1080 × 1920 px.
- All 8 launch graphics are 1080 × 1350 px.
- Every day from 1 through 30 has at least one finished asset.
- Carousel slides use visible numbering and progress indicators.
- Intermediate carousel slides no longer repeat the main CTA.
- Final carousel slides contain the website action.
- Story and reel actions sit above the lower interface-control area.
- Story production instructions are not included in the audience-facing artwork.
- Poll, quiz, and question layouts contain usable interaction zones.
- Body text, buttons, and URLs have been increased for phone viewing.
- Urdu text uses joined Nastaliq rendering and Urdu calls to action.
- The active-danger slide directs the reader to local emergency support rather than DilSe chat.
- No clipped headlines, broken glyphs, or overlapping Urdu text remain.

- numbered carousel steps;
- interactive story frames;
- reel covers with conversation previews.
