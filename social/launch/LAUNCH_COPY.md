## 03. Family context

**Caption**

A disagreement between two people can carry expectations from parents, in-laws, children, faith, finances, and family reputation.

DilSe asks about your circumstances instead of assuming every South Asian relationship works the same way. It helps you prepare words that fit your relationship, your boundaries, and the family context around you.

Visit www.baatdilse.com and start a conversation.

**Alt text:** Teal quote card saying that some conversations feel as if the whole family is in the room.

## 04. Conversation opener

**Caption**

A specific request names the area, the owner, and the work included. DilSe can help you draft a version that sounds natural in your voice, then practise the conversation before you begin.

Save this opener and adapt it to your household.

**Alt text:** A sample conversation opener asking a partner to take full ownership of school notices, forms, fees, and follow-up.

## Publishing notes

- Launch with posts 01 and 02 pinned to the profile.
- Publish post 03 two or three days later to establish the cultural position.
- Use post 04 as the first post in a repeatable “Words to practise” series.
- Do not describe DilSe as therapy or promise that conversations are confidential. The current product stores conversations and permits authorized administrator review under its Terms.
- Use the same closing action throughout the campaign: “Visit www.baatdilse.com and start a conversation.”
